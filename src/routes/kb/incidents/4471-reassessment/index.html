Shared parser

The shared parser reads month-first while these tenants write dates day-first. For example, R-41200 from harbour.dental reports a reminder for 7 April arriving for 4 July. The tenant directory identifies its locale as en-GB.

After excluding the desk-noted one-day moves, assign day-first tenant reports here except form-channel reports using booking-v3. Use the tenant's stored locale, not the reporter's browser locale or intake tag. Fix and test parsing separately from the displayed form hint.

Booking-v3 date hint

Booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY, but its form reads the date using the tenant's convention. For day-first tenants, following that hint can swap the intended date. R-41230 from copper.studio reports 1 June instead of 6 January; the tenant uses es-ES and booking-v3, and the report channel is form.

This group is limited to form-channel reports for day-first booking-v3 tenants. Their app and email reports stay in the parser group unless flagged as one-day moves. Align the hint with the accepted input and test both together; a parser fix alone does not demonstrate this mismatch is resolved.

One-day/timing shifts

The desk notes flag R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344 and R-41620. These reports are tracked separately in issue #4, not counted again in the corrected parser group.

R-41202 describes Tuesday 12 May becoming Monday 11 May. R-41344 reports:

Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting. We held it twice.

This is a different symptom from exchanging day and month. Investigate timezone conversion and date-boundary handling separately. R-41236 describes an invite arriving before the booking rather than an explicit one-day shift; its membership follows the desk note. The exact implementation defect is unverified, so the ticket's timezone label is an investigation direction, not a proven diagnosis for every report.
